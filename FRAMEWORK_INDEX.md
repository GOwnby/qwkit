## How the delivery is organized
The framework comes as numbered zips (`tools/package_framework.py`); unzip them all into one folder.
- `1-roadmap.zip`: this index, the open items, literature status, notes and docs, and the qwkit package with tests
  and tools (everything the papers need to run).
- `2-papers.zip`, `3-papers.zip`, ...: one paper each, in the order of the table below: its PDF, LaTeX source and
  study folder. A new paper gets the next number.

## What changed on 15 October 2026

1. **MAT-03 data at 5 V verified** three ways (Fig. 3b marker centroids, Fig. 4, Fig. 5a overlay): agreement within
   1.5%. Two errors elsewhere corrected (4 V mobility at 10 K; 6 V). Details: `studies/mat03/digitization/VERIFICATION.md`.
   All MAT-03 numbers regenerated from `studies/mat03/lee_fig3b_v2.json`; changes are at most a few per cent.
2. **The trapping explanation of MAT-03 did not survive verification.** At fixed total charge, moving the missing
   electrons into traps lowers the Fermi level by 0.1-0.3 eV (7-8 V); a trap could follow only with an entropy above
   10 k_B already between 2 and 10 K, and no physical trap spectrum works with fewer states than the interface has unit
   cells (`studies/mat03/trapping.py`). The alternative of a fixed slow fraction is in tension with the reported Hall
   linearity at 8 V (`localization.py`, new Fig. 3). The paper now keeps the firm 2 K result (an immobile quarter at
   7 V) and states that its origin is open.
3. **METH-01 placed** as a standalone methods note; the software paper cites it and was brought to 0.6.2.
4. **N2 drafted (roadmap P2.2, zip 12).** Freely programmed electrode biases against the best periodic electrical
   QPM, with real electrode fields and a breakdown limit: 1.2 times (ADQW) to 1.4 times (TW) at the design energy,
   1.1-1.8 times a retuned fixed device across 50 meV of pump tuning. qwkit 0.7.0 adds `propagate_path` and
   `field_profile` (69 tests). N2 also showed that N1's electrode-efficiency scaling must be replaced (LATER_STAGE 51).

## Rebuilding
- Tests: `python3 tests/run_tests.py`
- A study: `bash studies/rerun_<id>.sh` (N1, NL-02, NL-05, MS-02, TPV-05, MAT-01, MAT-02) or
  `bash studies/mat03/rerun_mat03.sh` / `studies/meth01/rerun_meth01.sh` / `studies/n2/rerun_n2.sh` (MAT-03 about 1.8 h, N2 about 8 h on 2 cores), then `pdflatex main && bibtex main && pdflatex main && pdflatex main` in the paper folder.
